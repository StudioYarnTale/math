🏰 The Kingdom of Numbers

Story 3.
New Residents of the Kingdom

That day in the 🏰 Kingdom of Numbers was very lively. 🦊Liumy had been running around the yard since early morning with his pockets full of 🌰nuts.

— Anastasia! — he shouted. — Look how many residents we have made! He carefully placed 🌰nuts along a long line. — One nut is the number 1, two nuts are the number 2, three nuts are 3…

Anastasia smiled: — These residents are called natural numbers. They are easy to show with nuts. 🦊Liumy kept placing 🌰nuts farther and farther… But suddenly he stopped. He put his paw into one pocket, then into the other. — Anastasia… the nuts are gone…

He thought for a moment, but he still very much wanted to continue playing. Suddenly his eyes lit up! 🦊The little fox jumped up and ran somewhere behind the house. A minute later he came back — breathless but very pleased. — I borrowed 🌰nuts from Auntie Polya! — he announced joyfully.

Without wasting any time, 🦊Liumy began placing nuts on the other side of zero. One… two… three… And he started writing: 1, 2, 3. But Anastasia immediately stopped him: — You cannot do that!

🦊Liumy looked surprised: — Why not? Anastasia pointed to the right side of the line: — We already have 1, 2, 3. They live over here. She moved her finger to the right. — And if numbers go in this direction, — she said, pointing to the left, — then they must be written with the sign «−».

She carefully wrote: −1, −2, −3. — These numbers are called negative numbers, — said Anastasia. 🦊Liumy frowned. He looked carefully at the new numbers and suddenly asked: — Negative… because they are bad? Do they behave badly?

Anastasia laughed brightly: — No, of course not bad! She shook her head: — That is simply how Grandfather explained it to me. And all together — both positive and negative — are called integers.

🦊Liumy listened carefully, twitching his ears. Anastasia continued: — After all, these are the 🌰nuts that we did not have at first. You borrowed them from Auntie Polya. Look… if you borrow 💰coins, you have to return them later, don't you?

🦊Liumy nodded eagerly: — Of course! I must return them! — Exactly the same here, — Anastasia nodded. — If you owe one coin — that is −1. If two — that is −2.

🦊Liumy nodded with satisfaction: — So… negative numbers are like debts! He looked again at the number line. Now it seemed even longer — because numbers lived not only to the right of zero, but also to the left. And that meant… 🏰 The Kingdom of Numbers had become twice as large! 🌟
